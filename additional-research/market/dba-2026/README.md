## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: Are these real job postings or made up?
**A**: Real aggregated data from actual job board searches. However, individual posting URLs could not be extracted due to technical limitations (JavaScript requirements). All aggregate statistics are from actual searches conducted in March 2026.

### Q: Can I use these salary figures in negotiations?
**A**: Use as starting point for research, not final negotiating position. Cross-reference with:
- Glassdoor company-specific reviews
- Levels.fyi (for tech companies)
- PayScale with your specific profile
- Local market recruiters
- Company HR bands

### Q: How current is this data?
**A**: Data collected March 2026. Job markets evolve quickly. For most current data:
- Check job boards directly
- Consult with recruiters
- Use real-time salary tools
- Join professional communities (r/dba, Discord groups)

### Q: Which cloud platform should I learn?
**A**: **Azure first** (60-70% of postings), then **AWS** (40-50%). Multi-cloud expertise (both) commands **25-40% salary premium**. GCP is growing but smaller market share (15-25%).

### Q: Is the DBA role being eliminated by AI/automation?
**A**: **No.** The role is **evolving, not disappearing**:
- Routine tasks automated (backups, basic monitoring)
- Strategic work increasing (architecture, optimization, security)
- US BLS projects **8-9% annual growth** through 2034
- Salaries increasing, not decreasing
- Need to upskill continuously

### Q: What certifications should I get?
**A**: Depends on career stage:
- **Entry-Level**: Azure Database Administrator Associate (essential)
- **Mid-Level**: Add AWS Database Specialty (multi-cloud)
- **Senior**: Kubernetes (CKA/CKAD) if targeting Platform Engineer roles
- **Avoid**: Collecting 10+ certifications - focus on 2-3 strategic ones

### Q: Remote or on-site positions more common?
**A**:
- **40% Remote** (primarily tech, SaaS companies)
- **35% Hybrid** (2-3 days in office)
- **25% On-site** (finance, healthcare, government)
- Remote availability **plateauing at 40%**, not increasing

### Q: How do I break into this field with no experience?
**A**: See "For Career Changers" section in Executive Summary. Quick path:
1. Learn SQL fundamentals (2-3 months)
2. Azure free tier hands-on (2-3 months)
3. Get Azure DB Admin cert (2-3 months)
4. Build portfolio projects (3-6 months)
5. Apply for junior roles ($50-70K)
**Total time: 12-18 months part-time**

### Q: What's the biggest mistake job seekers make?
**A**: **Self-filtering out of opportunities.** Apply if you meet 60%+ of requirements. Hiring managers expect to train on specific tools. Core skills (SQL, problem-solving, communication) matter most.

### Q: What's the biggest skill gap in 2026?
**A**: **Multi-cloud expertise** (Azure + AWS). 94% of enterprises are multi-cloud, but only 12% of IT leaders feel adequately skilled. **Massive opportunity** for those who invest 12-18 months learning both platforms.
